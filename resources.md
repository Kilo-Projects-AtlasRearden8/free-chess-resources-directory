## Lichess

**Cost:** Free (100% free, no subscriptions, no ads)  
**Platform:** Web, iOS, Android  
**Steps:**  
1. Go to https://lichess.org  
2. Play instantly without an account, or sign up for free to save games  
3. Choose a time control (bullet, blitz, rapid, classical) and start a game  
4. Use the "Learn" menu for puzzles, tactics trainer, and studies  

**Official link:** https://lichess.org

## Chess.com (free tier)

**Cost:** Free tier (premium features exist but are not required)  
**Platform:** Web, iOS, Android  
**Steps:**  
1. Go to https://www.chess.com  
2. Create a free account  
3. Play daily games and puzzles within the free daily limits  
4. Explore the free "Learn" lessons and puzzle rush  

**Official link:** https://www.chess.com

**Cost:** Free  
**Platform:** Web, iOS, Android
